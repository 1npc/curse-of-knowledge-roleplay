# Word guessing task

Thank you for helping with a language research study. It takes about 20 to 30 minutes.

**Before you start, please answer three questions:**

1. Is English the language you use most in daily life? (yes / no)
2. Can you speak or understand any Chinese, Japanese, or Spanish, even a little? If yes, which? 
3. Can you read Chinese characters (hanzi or kanji)? (yes / no)

**Instructions.** Each question shows one foreign word, sometimes with how it sounds in Latin letters. Pick the English meaning you think is most likely. Please do not look anything up, use a translator, or ask anyone. If you have no idea, just pick the option that feels closest; guessing is expected.

Your answers are anonymous and used only to decide which words are too easy to guess. You can stop at any time.
